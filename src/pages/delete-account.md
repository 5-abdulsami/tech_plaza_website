## How to request deletion

1. Send an email to **[support@techplaza.app](mailto:support@techplaza.app?subject=Delete%20my%20Tech%20Plaza%20account)** from the email address you use to sign in to Tech Plaza.
2. Use the subject **“Delete my account”**. If you run a shop, include your shop name.
3. We may reply to confirm the request is really from you.
4. We delete your account within **30 days** and email you when it’s done.

Signing in with Google? Send the email from that Google account’s address.

## What we delete

- Your account and profile: name, email, phone number and profile photo
- Your favourites, the shops you follow and the ratings you gave
- Your push notification token
- For shop owners: your shop, products, photos, CNIC details and CNIC image

## What we may keep

- Information we must keep by law, or to deal with fraud or a dispute
- **Messages** you sent to other people may stay in their chat history

> Deleting your account can’t be undone. If you only want to stop receiving notifications, turn off **Push notifications** in the app’s Settings instead.

See our [Privacy policy](/privacy) for more about how we handle your data.
